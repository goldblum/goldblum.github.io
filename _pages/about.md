I am an assistant professor at Columbia University EE and CS, and I co-founded the [Columbia Core AI Lab (CAIL)](https://cail.columbia.edu/). My research spans both applied and fundamental problems in machine learning, including training and inference strategies for large-scale models, AI safety, and automated machine learning and research. I have also worked on probabilistic deep learning, generalization theory, algorithm discovery, and AI security and privacy. Across these areas, my aim is to build a mathematical and scientific understanding of why complex AI systems work.

Before joining Columbia, I was a postdoc at New York University, working with [Yann LeCun](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=WLN3QrAAAAAJ) and [Andrew Gordon Wilson](https://cims.nyu.edu/~andrewgw/). Our paper on model comparison received the Outstanding Paper Award at ICML 2022.  I received my Ph.D. in mathematics from the University of Maryland, where I worked with [Tom Goldstein](https://www.cs.umd.edu/~tomg/) and [Wojciech Czaja](https://www.math.umd.edu/~czaja/).
